The Planets

Meet the eight major planets of our Solar System.

Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun. Mercury has a rocky surface and extreme temperature changes.

🌡️ Very hot days and cold nights
☀️ Closest to the Sun

Venus

Venus is Earth's neighboring planet and has a thick, carbon-dioxide-rich atmosphere.

🔥 Hottest planet
☁️ Thick atmosphere

Earth

Our home planet. Earth has liquid water on its surface and supports a huge variety of life.

🌊 Lots of liquid water
🌱 Known life

Mars

Known as the Red Planet because iron minerals in its soil give its surface a reddish appearance.

🔴 Red Planet
🏔️ Home to Olympus Mons

Jupiter

The largest planet in our Solar System. Jupiter is a gas giant famous for its Great Red Spot.

🌪️ Great Red Spot
🪐 Largest planet

Saturn

A giant planet famous for its spectacular system of icy rings.

💍 Famous ring system
🌌 Gas giant

Uranus

An ice giant with a blue-green color caused by methane in its atmosphere.

🧊 Ice giant
↩️ Rotates on its side

Neptune

The farthest recognized major planet from the Sun and a world of powerful winds.

💨 Extremely windy
🔵 Deep blue appearance
